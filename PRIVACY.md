# Privacy Policy

Perks Reminder respects your privacy. The application stores only the data necessary to provide core functionality.

- No ads, no sale of personal data
- Authentication via Google OAuth (email and basic profile info)
- User-provided data: credit cards, benefits, loyalty accounts
- Email notifications sent via Resend (uses your email)
- The optional browser reader reads only named first-party American Express benefit endpoints after you start a manual scan while signed in. It does not inspect passwords, cookies, MFA values, authorization headers, or opaque account tokens, and it does not save raw provider responses.
- The reader stores normalized benefit observations and a local installation fingerprint in browser extension or userscript storage. You can clear this local data from the reader panel; clearing removes the observations and identity secret.
- Choosing **Sync reviewed** sends only the validated normalized handoff to the Perks Reminder site for its separate preview/confirmation flow. No handoff occurs automatically, and no provider data is sold, used for ads, analytics, or shared with unrelated parties.
- Install either the Chrome extension or the Greasy Fork userscript, not both; both are alternate distributions of the same in-page reader.

Exports: You can export/import your data under `Settings → Data`.

Questions? Open an issue or use the contact page in the app.
